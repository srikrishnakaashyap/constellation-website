Team memory

When one person corrects something, everyone gets the correction.

People know things no website says. Constellation uses your team's memory (through OpenCrawl) as a witness. A correction is made once, applied to the shared graph, and used to check every answer and draft after it.

Marketing corrects

"We discontinued the Moon Boot."

  1. LIVE TODAYA marketer leaves a note.

    "Moon Boot is discontinued. Never mention it." The note sits in the team's memory, not in a slide deck.

  2. LIVE TODAYThe graph is corrected.

    The fact-check reads the note, flags the Moon Boot node, and flags every draft built on it, with the note as the reason.

  3. NEXTThe next person who asks is told.

    A teammate asks about featuring it in Friday's post and hears: "Not any more. Marketing marked it discontinued. Here is what changed."

Engineering updates

"One-click pay ships in January, not December."

  1. LIVE TODAYEngineering notes the change.

    The note is picked up as team knowledge, so a claim that says "December" is checked against it.

  2. NEXTIt reaches Sales and Marketing.

    Anyone whose work touches the old date is shown the update: the pitch, the post, the support answer.

  3. NEXTEveryone answers the same way.

    Sales asks "When does one-click pay launch?" and gets January, with who changed it and when.

Someone is suddenly out

"I'm out sick. Here is where everything stands."

  1. NEXTThey mark themselves out.

    One click. Constellation gathers what they were working on: open drafts, scheduled workflows, pending approvals, and the claims they were checking.

  2. NEXTA handoff brief is built.

    A short, sourced summary of each item, what is done, what is waiting, and what changed. Private notes stay private unless they choose to share them.

  3. NEXTA teammate picks it up.

    They open the shared brief, ask follow-up questions against the same graph, and continue with nothing lost in a chat thread or someone's head.

Private by design. Only team memories and connected sources are read, never raw chat transcripts. Notes inform checks and are never quoted in public output. Examples above are illustrative.
Under the hood

How the map gets built and kept honest.

The same five steps run for every team. What changes is the question each team asks.

  1. STEP 1

    Ingest

    Crawl the site, find the social accounts it links to, and parse uploaded documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD).

  2. STEP 2

    Extract

    An LLM pulls typed entities and relations. Each one carries its source and a confidence.

  3. STEP 3

    Fact-check

    A strict pass re-reads every node at its live source. A hallucinated node is detached and kept as proof.

  4. STEP 4

    Watch

    Radar searches live news and regulation, then scores each item against your graph in code.

  5. STEP 5

    Act

    Agents draft cited actions. A person approves, edits or dismisses each one.

Node types: Company · Product · Audience · Value Prop · Brand Voice · Social Account · Post · Claim · Regulation · Competitor · Theme · Passage
